About timing accuracy

Your computer adds a small, unknown delay between the string moving and the browser hearing it. That means consistency — whether your notes are evenly spaced — is always measured correctly, but rushing or dragging is only as accurate as the delay estimate below. Headphones are strongly recommended: through speakers the microphone hears the click itself and counts it as a note.
